FAQs

What is AI TEXTly?

AI TEXTly estimates whether a piece of text was written by a person or generated by an AI, and shows a probability instead of a yes-or-no verdict.

How does it work?

A model trained on tens of thousands of human and AI answers learns the differences in wording and style. Long texts are split into chunks, each chunk is scored, and the scores are combined. This site is currently running the {{ detector_name }} model.

Is it accurate?

On our held-out test set the models score about 95–96% accuracy. That test mostly covers ChatGPT-3.5 and GPT-3 style writing, so newer AI models, edited AI text and very short texts can be misjudged. No AI detector is perfect: treat the score as a signal, not as proof.

What languages does it support?

English only.

Do you store my text?

No. AI TEXTly does not save the text you submit; it is analysed and discarded when the result is sent back.
